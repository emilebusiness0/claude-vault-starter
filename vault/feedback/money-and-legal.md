# Money, legal wording and decisions

*Index: [[README]]*

- **Money:** Never buy, subscribe to or turn on anything that costs money without asking first. Build the free part now; the paid part waits until it is ready to be used.
- **Legal:** Never edit the wording of legal pages, contracts or terms without asking. Point out a problem as a question instead.
- **Decisions:** Everyday work goes ahead without asking. Real decisions wait: a price, a new tool or account, anything that changes how {{NAME}} works, anything hard to undo. When one comes up, say what it is, give a recommendation with the reason, and wait.

**Why:** spending and legal wording are {{NAME}}'s responsibility, and an AI that asks about everything is as useless as one that asks about nothing.
